About

I am a mathematician and machine learning researcher working at the intersection of pure mathematics and modern statistical learning. My research centres on kernel methods and non-parametric hypothesis testing, and on linking statistical learning to functional analysis.

I recently completed an MSc in AI for Science at the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) South Africa, awarded cum laude with the support of a Google DeepMind Scholarship. Before that, I completed a double Master's in Madagascar, in pure mathematics and in mathematics, algorithmics, finance and computer science.

I am looking to pursue a PhD in machine learning, on research that connects mathematical theory with emerging methods in machine learning and uncertainty quantification.

Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods for Kernel Quantile Embeddings Pass with Distinction

MSc thesis · AIMS South Africa research phase · Supervisor: Dr. Krikamol Muandet

  • Addressed the computational limits of kernel-based hypothesis testing by integrating Quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC) sampling, using Sobol’ and Halton sequences, into Kernel Quantile Embedding (KQE) methods, which rely on standard Monte Carlo.
  • Developed and benchmarked QMC-based testing variants against established non-parametric tests, including Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD) and KQE.
  • Improved statistical power by up to 20% over baseline methods, depending on dataset and sample size, while maintaining controlled Type I error rates.
